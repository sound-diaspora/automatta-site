AutoMatta

AutoMatta is a private, single-user automation app owned and operated by Chad Matta (Kryptek LLC). It is not offered to the public.

What it does

AutoMatta connects Chad Matta's personal assistant software, which runs on his own computer, to his own Google account. With his authorization it reads and organizes his own email, calendar and files, and updates his own documents and spreadsheets, so the assistant can prepare briefings, track job applications and keep his notes current.

Who can use it

Only the owner's Google account is authorized. No other person's data is requested, collected or processed.
